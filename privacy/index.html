Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This policy describes data processing by the internal utility Firebase Crashlytics WeCom Alerts (the “Utility”).

Purpose

The Utility helps authorized engineering and operations personnel monitor Firebase Crashlytics alerts for an Android smart-locker application.

Google account data accessed

With the Google account holder's authorization, the Utility searches Gmail for Firebase Crashlytics notification messages and reads relevant message details, including sender, subject, body, date and Firebase issue links. The OAuth scope used by the current Apps Script implementation permits broader Gmail access; the Utility is designed to process only matching Crashlytics alerts.

Other diagnostic data

When available, the Utility reads Crashlytics issue events, which may include application versions, cabinet or device identifiers, exception messages, stack-frame locations, timestamps and other crash diagnostic metadata.

How data is used and shared

Relevant details are formatted into an alert and transmitted to the configured WeCom group via its robot webhook for operational troubleshooting. The WeCom service and authorized group participants receive the information included in those messages. The Utility does not sell Gmail data or use it for advertising.

Storage and retention

Google Apps Script properties retain limited message identifiers and processing timestamps to avoid duplicate notifications, plus configuration values necessary to operate the integration. The current automation is configured to process alert messages within a limited lookback period. Diagnostic notifications remain subject to the retention and access policies of the receiving WeCom organization; Gmail and Firebase retain their own records under their respective settings and policies.

Security

Access to the Apps Script project, Firebase project and WeCom group should be limited to authorized personnel. The WeCom webhook credential is stored in Apps Script properties rather than published on this website. No claim is made that transmissions or third-party storage are risk-free.

Changes

We may update this policy if the Utility's permissions, data practices or recipients change. The effective date above will be updated accordingly.
